Way out West

It is the spring of 1884. America's west coast is slowly being populated with small towns full of settlers, coming to prospect for precious metals, set up ranches and run dubious saloons. Now the railway is coming!

Cactus Gulch is one such small town. Founded 20 years ago, tonight it has a festive air as the townsfolk get set to start their 20th anniversary celebrations.

However, all is not running smoothly. Land disputes, disreputable card games, strange folk from out of town, and quarrels with the local Naptaya people are all adding to a tense atmosphere…

Way Out West Free is the complete 10-player version of one of our bestselling murder mystery games. Download it free to see how Freeform Games work—no scripts, individual objectives, secrets, alliances and multiple storylines.

Not your traditional murder mystery party game!

Way out West isn't a traditional murder mystery.

  • No script to read
  • Everyone has their own goals
  • The murderer knows they did it - and has goals of their own
  • Players can negotiate, bluff and scheme
  • Rules for combat and poison - some characters may die!
  • Multiple storylines
  • The ending is determined by the player

Cast of characters

  • Blaise Sadler – Aging saloon owner
  • Clem Parham – City slicker, Mid West Rail rep
  • Dan Fairweather – Slow and honest deputy sheriff
  • Doc Faraday – Notorious gambler
  • Elijah Entwhistle – Travelling preacher
  • John Paulson – Town's much-revered judge
  • Lucy Calhoun – Zeke's daughter, young woman, complete tomboy
  • Mel Easton – City slicker, South West Rail rep
  • Runs Like a Deer – Naptaya woman
  • Slick O'Hare – Scruffy ranch hand for hire

Historical note: The Native American people in this game, the Naptaya, are fictional—we thought it would be inappropriate to use the name of a real people of the area. At the time, the settlers would mostly have called them ‘Indians’. But of course you and your guests can refer to them as Naptaya or as Native Americans, as you prefer.

  15. Rated 2 out of 5

    Noah (verified owner) – 6 October 2025

    We just did the free version of Way Out West on 10/04/2025. First and foremost, let me say I’m giving it a 2 because of issues I’ll note in the review. The system itself is great and I like the core ideas I’m running one of the Halloween games in a couple weeks for a larger group. Overall, I appreciate the company and will be giving them more business, and we as a group are already looking at ways to expand the system to add some new twists and allow for legacy play where we can have multiple games where less character introduction is needed and folks can be the same character more than once.

    As to the 10 player version. A few things certainly caused issues that felt a bit silly. Firstly, the card game is one of the major pivot points for interaction and roleplay. However there at least half the characters begin the game already knowing what happened and who has the evidence to prove it. One of the chacters primary objective is prevent the truth from coming out but that is nearly impossible to do. You’d need to kill or bribe off too many people immediately which you simply cannot do. The player who played that particular character was then completely off the rails by the time deaths went active. Similarly, a killer’s entire game depends on them hiding their identity but again 4 players start not only knowing they aren’t who they claim but know exactly who they are. Another character, who is probably their biggest threat, is given that information very early in the game. That information would be a great twist if they were given it in the last hour and if literally everyone didn’t know who he was within the 1st 30 minutes.

    On top of that, the mystery was a little weak in that identifying the guilty parties was so easy as to almost be arbitrary while the actual circumstances of the murder were in fact nearly impossible to actually solve completely. There is no actual evidence to determine the cause of death, and that cause of death has no bearing on solving the crime. Halfway through the game someone actually said “if it turns out that “x” happened, that will be a super unsatisfying reveal.” Everyone else agreed. The problem being that they actually correctly blind-guessed the answer.

    Additionally there are two items that aren’t properly explained. They appear to act as a weapon but aren’t labeled as a weapon so we assumed they just work when used. But that ended up being extremely strong and unbalanced.

    All in all its complicated. The characters are very good. They are interesting have have good motivations. The abilities appear to fairly balanced although they could be improved a bit. Some of the items could be cleaned up a bit as well. I’d give all that like a B+.

    However, I think the pacing of information was not well thought out. The fact that it so heavily revealed certain characters info that the game suffers for it. To me that drags this all the way down to a D-. To be completely honest if I didn’t have such a great group I would have been a little sad that I had put so much work into it.
